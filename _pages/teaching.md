My teaching connects security, intelligent systems, and hands-on experimentation. I have developed and taught courses across cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, systems, networking, data analytics, and software development.

## Southern Illinois University Carbondale

- **AI Safety and Society**
- **Ethics, Safety, and Security of AI**
- **Ethical Hacking**
- **Digital Forensics**
- **Cyber-Physical Systems**
- **Security in Cyber-Physical Systems**
- **Wireless and Network Security**

I also contributed to the development of SIU's **B.S. in AI+**, including courses in AI ethics, safety, security, and prompt engineering.

## Previous teaching

At Robert Morris University and Concord University, I taught courses in cybersecurity, intelligent systems, data mining, data warehousing, operating systems, networking, web development, parallel and distributed computing, human-centered design, simulation, algorithms, and capstone design.

## Experiential education

I have mentored participants in NSF Research Experiences for Undergraduates and Research Experiences for Teachers, and I integrate smartphones, sensors, wearables, edge devices, generative AI, and security testbeds into student projects.
