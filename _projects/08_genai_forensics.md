This project examines privacy and forensic questions arising from consumer generative AI systems. Topics include identity and attribute inference from transformed writing, health inference from cloud whiteboards, audio editing as a user-controlled privacy defense, and forensic investigation of generative AI applications.

The goal is to create practical methods for assessing whether AI transformations meaningfully protect users and whether AI-assisted activity can be reconstructed and audited.
