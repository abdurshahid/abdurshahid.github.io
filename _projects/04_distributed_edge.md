This program develops privacy-preserving and resource-efficient distributed learning for wearable activity recognition, multimodal stress detection, healthcare, autonomous systems, UAV imagery, and heterogeneous edge environments.

Research challenges include non-IID data, malicious participants, adversarial training, communication constraints, data sovereignty, and cross-device deployment. Earlier work in this program includes differential privacy for sensing and healthcare, spatiotemporal location privacy, and Sensor-Chain, a lightweight blockchain architecture for mobile and sensing-based IoT systems.
