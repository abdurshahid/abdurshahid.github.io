ConsumerGenEdit studies edits produced by real consumer-device AI tools rather than only research models or synthetic editing pipelines. The benchmark covers original and edited image pairs, multiple platforms and device families, diverse edit operations, multilingual descriptions, localization metadata, and JPEG AI compression variants.

The project supports research in edit detection, operation classification, localization, cross-platform generalization, multimedia forensics, content authenticity, and multimodal AI.
