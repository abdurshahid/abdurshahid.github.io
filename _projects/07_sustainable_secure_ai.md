Security mechanisms can improve robustness while increasing computation, energy use, latency, and environmental impact. This project develops measurement frameworks for adversarial training, federated learning, and edge AI to quantify these trade-offs.

The work includes carbon-emission measurement, social-cost analysis, resource-amplification attacks, and defense strategies designed for sustainable deployment.
