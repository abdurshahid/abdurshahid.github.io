This program studies attacks against wearable, federated, medical, multimodal, and cyber-physical AI. Current directions include data poisoning, label flipping, context-aware spatiotemporal attacks, adversarial vision-language inputs, cross-modal sensor–text attacks, and energy-latency sponge attacks.

We develop and evaluate defenses based on model pruning, language-model-assisted detection and sanitization, privacy mechanisms, and robust distributed learning. A central theme is understanding how security choices affect accuracy, latency, energy consumption, carbon emissions, and social cost in resource-constrained and safety-critical systems.
