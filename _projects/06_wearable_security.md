This project examines how malicious or corrupted inputs affect wearable AI across sensing, multimodal fusion, language-model reasoning, and distributed learning. It includes poisoning attacks against activity recognition, cross-modal sensor–text manipulation, energy-latency attacks, and LLM-assisted detection and sanitization.

The long-term goal is a modular security architecture for wearable AI that combines sensing, local reasoning, federated coordination, and resource-aware defense.
