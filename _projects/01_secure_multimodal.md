This program develops architectures that integrate wearable and environmental sensors, smartphones, edge devices, vision-language models, and large or small language models. The goal is to enable reliable sensing-to-reasoning pipelines for applications such as human activity recognition, healthcare, worker safety, emergency response, threat intelligence, and cyber-physical systems.

We study vulnerabilities across the full pipeline, including compromised sensor data, cross-modal misalignment, poisoning-induced reasoning drift, adversarial visual inputs, and unsafe downstream actions. Our work explores detection, sanitization, containment, and resource-aware defenses suitable for edge and wearable deployment.

**Representative projects:** wearable AI poisoning detection, cross-modal sensor–text attacks, fall detection and emergency response with small language models, WatchOverGPT, multimodal edge-cloud agents, and healthcare CPS architectures.
